A free, debloated LineageOS 17.1 custom ROM for the OPPO A5s (model CPH1909, also sold as OPPO AX5s), replacing ColorOS with a clean Android 10 build. Everything important is baked into the system image — a factory reset costs you about two minutes of re-setup, not a re-flash.

What this custom ROM fixes on the OPPO A5s

  • Instagram & Facebook audio — a full xHE-AAC decoder compiled from AOSP libxaac is grafted into the media stack; Meta's modern audio streams decode correctly (stock firmware-era decoders crash on them).
  • Vendor crash-loops — 8 linker stubs repair the boot-time CANNOT-LINK failures of the fingerprint/face/soter services on GSIs.
  • Bloat removed — 30+ apps stripped; Google Play + services only; the Contacts provider stays, the UI goes.
  • Battery & memory — 1.5 GB zram swap and GMS battery optimization applied every boot; VNDK trimmed by ~360 MB.
  • Magisk root — patched boot image included; no modules required.

Requirements

An unlocked bootloader, a Windows PC, and about an hour. The flashing uses MediaTek BROM mode via mtkclient — no fastboot needed. The complete guide covers backups, flashing, TWRP, setup, and troubleshooting.

Known limits (honesty section)

Fingerprint and face unlock are dead on this generation of GSIs (vendor trustlet); the OS UI battery-temperature reading is a MediaTek naming quirk; no Vulkan driver exists for this SoC. Everything else a daily driver needs works.
